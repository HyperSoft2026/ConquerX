Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026 · Last updated: October 18, 2026
Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information ConquerX may collect, why we use it, how we protect it, and how you can request access or deletion.

1. Who We Are

ConquerX is operated by the ConquerX team (“ConquerX”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). For privacy questions, account requests, or deletion requests, contact modeali2021@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Guest access

Guest mode is designed for temporary play. We do not create a permanent online account for a guest solely because they play as a guest. Some temporary technical information may be processed when the game communicates with our servers, such as request information needed to provide and secure the service. Guest progress is not intended to be permanently associated with an identifiable account.

Account-based sign-in

When you sign in with a supported identity provider, such as Discord, we may receive limited profile information made available through the provider’s authorization flow, such as a provider user identifier and basic profile information required to identify your ConquerX account.

Game information

For registered accounts, we may store game information such as player identifier, progress, level, statistics, kills, wins, virtual currency balances, unlocked cosmetic items, leaderboard information, preferences, and account timestamps.

Support and business communications

If you contact us, we may receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address, account identifier, and the details of your request.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and authenticate ConquerX accounts.
  • To save and restore eligible game progress.
  • To provide multiplayer, matchmaking, leaderboard, and account features.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, investigate technical problems, and protect the service.
  • To respond to support, privacy, account, business, and advertising inquiries.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve ConquerX.

4. Discord Data

If you choose Discord sign-in, ConquerX uses Discord’s authorized authentication flow. We do not request, collect, or store your Discord password. We only request information necessary for the stated ConquerX account functionality and handle Discord-derived information according to this Privacy Policy and applicable requirements.

Discord independently processes information under its own privacy policy and terms. ConquerX does not control Discord’s independent processing.

5. Future Sign-In Providers

ConquerX may add other sign-in providers, such as Google, Apple, or Microsoft. If a provider is added, we will update this Privacy Policy as needed to explain the relevant information collected and how it is used. We do not claim to collect data from a provider before that integration is actually enabled.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when necessary to operate ConquerX through service providers that process information on our behalf, to comply with applicable law or a valid legal request, to protect the security and integrity of the service, or when you expressly direct us to do so.

We do not use Discord API data for unrelated purposes. We also do not use Discord-derived information for targeted advertising or profiling unrelated to the operation of ConquerX.

7. Data Retention

We retain account and game information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested account and game services, maintain security, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements. When information is no longer required, we may delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your ConquerX account, subject to applicable law and reasonable steps to verify the request. We will process valid requests within the period required by applicable law.

For an account deletion request, email modeali2021@gmail.com with the subject “ConquerX Account Deletion Request”. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, or other secret credentials.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, so please keep your device and account credentials secure.

10. Children and Age Requirements

ConquerX is intended to be used in accordance with applicable age requirements and laws. We do not knowingly request unnecessary sensitive information from children. If you believe information was provided to ConquerX in a manner that violates applicable age or privacy requirements, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

11. Third-Party Services

ConquerX may rely on third-party infrastructure and authentication services. These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We aim to use only the information reasonably necessary for the ConquerX features they support.

12. International Processing

Depending on our hosting, infrastructure, and service providers, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable, we will use appropriate safeguards required by law.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when ConquerX changes, new features are introduced, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates the current version.
